Songsuz yash, songsuz üzüngtu.
Keldi yash, yüzde körungdu.
Le'emma kimanai seng ? kì
keldang sahtangdan mengha ?
— Men, tsamang. 
Utang ! Kì hong aldap senghì
alamdìng senghì, bildungmì ?
Ah ewlad, kentanì öyle emes,
alìnmaq hungdan zamang tes,

yokuysà kitersang,
yashlarghìng shi'irgha alang,
seng kitersang, 
Humnai yangliz kalardamang..

-- 

Endless tears, endless sadiness.
It came (age), and been visible on (his) face.
But who are you ? That,
came from a sudden to me ?
— I, am the Time (moment, time, ... related to the time)
Shame (on you) ! Who got taken (killed, destroyed, bruised) by the Time!
That got taken (here, killed, destroyed, bruised) by it, didn't you know (been aware of it) ? [about the fact that time did exist]
O you son (My friend, little one, mate)! Don't make yourself like this! (~be aware that time exists)
[Don't make yourself like this] so time could not take you (~make you feel regrets!)

Otherwise you will go [away from me, this world, here]
And your tears will become [the first lines of] a text [poetic text, poetry, writing, wise says, says, ...]
[indeed] if you go [away from me],
I will feel loneliness in my beinginess [and won't be able to continue]
